How AssayLint handles your data

  • Processing: all parsing and checks run in this browser tab. Files are never uploaded; this site has no server-side code, accounts, analytics or telemetry.
  • Storage: experiments (including the original files you import) are saved in this browser's local storage on this device, for this site only. They are not encrypted by AssayLint; protection relies on your device account and disk encryption.
  • Access: only people who can use this browser profile can see the data. To share with a collaborator, export the experiment packet or report and send it through a channel you trust.
  • Retention and backup: data stays until you delete it or clear site data. There is no automatic backup; export the packet to keep a copy.
  • Deletion: “Delete experiment” on the Setup tab removes the experiment, its files, plate map, notes and audit history from this browser. Clearing site data removes everything.
  • No secondary use: your research data is never used for model training, benchmarking or sharing. There is nothing to opt into.

Glossary

Ct / Cq
Threshold or quantification cycle: the cycle at which fluorescence crosses a threshold. Lower means more starting template.
NTC
No-template control: a reaction without template. It should not amplify.
No-RT control
An RNA sample taken through the reaction without reverse transcriptase. Signal can indicate genomic DNA.
Positive control
A reaction expected to amplify, showing that the assay can work on this plate.
Technical replicate
Repeated wells of the same biological sample. They measure pipetting and assay precision, not biological variation.
Biological unit
An independent sample (for example one animal, culture or patient). Statistics across conditions should be based on these.
Standard curve
A dilution series of known quantities. Its slope gives the amplification efficiency; R² shows linearity.
Melt curve
Fluorescence while heating the product after cycling (intercalating dyes only). One peak suggests one product.
Finding disposition
Your review decision: accepted, not applicable, resolved or unresolved. It never changes the data or removes the original finding.
