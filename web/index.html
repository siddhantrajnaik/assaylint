Where your data goes

Nowhere. Everything runs in your browser. There's no server behind this site, so I never see your files or results.

  • Saved where: in this browser, on this computer. Another browser or computer won't see it.
  • Who can see it: anyone who uses this browser profile. It isn't encrypted beyond whatever your computer already does.
  • Sharing: download the report or experiment packet and send it however you normally would.
  • Backups: there aren't any. Download the experiment packet if you want a copy.
  • Deleting: use “Delete experiment” on the Setup tab, or clear this site's data in your browser to remove everything.
  • Tracking: none. No analytics, no accounts, and your data is never used for anything else.

Glossary

Ct / Cq
Threshold or quantification cycle: the cycle at which fluorescence crosses a threshold. Lower means more starting template.
NTC
No-template control: a reaction without template. It should not amplify.
No-RT control
An RNA sample taken through the reaction without reverse transcriptase. Signal can indicate genomic DNA.
Positive control
A reaction expected to amplify, showing that the assay can work on this plate.
Technical replicate
Repeated wells of the same biological sample. They measure pipetting and assay precision, not biological variation.
Biological unit
An independent sample (for example one animal, culture or patient). Statistics across conditions should be based on these.
Standard curve
A dilution series of known quantities. Its slope gives the amplification efficiency; R² shows linearity.
Melt curve
Fluorescence while heating the product after cycling (intercalating dyes only). One peak suggests one product.
Finding disposition
Your review decision: accepted, not applicable, resolved or unresolved. It never changes the data or removes the original finding.
