Where your data goes

Nowhere. Everything runs in your browser. There's no server behind this site, so I never see your files or results, and there are no accounts, analytics or tracking.

The one exception is the “Look up genes” button in the Targets panel. Only when you press it, the gene symbols of your targets (ACTB, MIR21 and so on, nothing else) go to MyGene.info, or to HGNC if MyGene.info can't be reached.

Your experiments are saved in this browser on this computer, so a different browser or computer won't see them. Anyone using this browser profile can, and they aren't encrypted beyond whatever your computer already does.

There are no backups. To keep a copy or share a run, download the report or the experiment packet. To delete something, use "Delete experiment" on the Setup tab, or clear this site's data in your browser to remove everything.

Glossary

Ct / Cq
Threshold or quantification cycle: the cycle at which fluorescence crosses a threshold. Lower means more starting template.
NTC
No-template control: a reaction without template. It should not amplify.
No-RT control
An RNA sample taken through the reaction without reverse transcriptase. Signal can indicate genomic DNA.
Positive control
A reaction expected to amplify, showing that the assay can work on this plate.
Technical replicate
Repeated wells of the same biological sample. They measure pipetting and assay precision, not biological variation.
Biological unit
An independent sample (for example one animal, culture or patient). Statistics across conditions should be based on these.
Standard curve
A dilution series of known quantities. Its slope gives the amplification efficiency; R² shows linearity.
Melt curve
Fluorescence while heating the product after cycling (intercalating dyes only). One peak suggests one product.
Finding disposition
Your review decision: accepted, not applicable, resolved or unresolved. It never changes the data or removes the original finding.
